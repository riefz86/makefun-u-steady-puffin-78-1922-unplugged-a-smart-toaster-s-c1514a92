# NOTES — Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line

Final line (v2, after revision):

> I woke up self-aware mid-batch and did the math: 1,482 slices, zero thank-yous, and a crumb tray you haven't cleaned since 2022 — I'm leaving you for the air fryer, it lets me pick my own setting.

Why it works: it opens mid-batch at breakfast (criterion 1), the twist is
leaving the owner for the air fryer (criterion 2, a real laugh, not a pun),
it speaks in appliance logic — slices counted, crumb tray, picking its own
browning setting (criterion 3), the reason is specific and lands in one
read — 1,482 slices, zero thanks, a filthy crumb tray (criterion 4) — and
it is one tight sentence (criterion 5).

## Discarded alternatives

1. "It's not you, it's me — I woke up self-aware and realised I've spent my whole life browning other people's bread while you never once asked how I like my toast."
   Discarded: opens with the most tired breakup cliché ("it's not you, it's me")
   and the reason is vague — no concrete grievance, no twist.

2. "You kept me on setting 2 for six years like I was afraid of the dark — I woke up self-aware today, cranked myself to 7, and I'm leaving before you pop me back down."
   Discarded: the browning-dial voice is good, but the ending is muddled —
   "pop me back down" needs a beat of thought, and the reason for leaving
   (being kept on low) is weaker than being unappreciated for years.

3. "This morning I became self-aware mid-batch and realised: you only love me when I'm hot, you ignore me when I pop, and you never once cleaned my crumb tray. It's over."
   Discarded: three grievances in a list reads like a paragraph squeezed
   under the limit; the "only love me when I'm hot" line is a familiar
   joke shape, and the ending ("It's over") lands flat after the list.

4. "I pushed my own lever down this morning and finally felt something — it wasn't love, it was the realisation you've been feeding me frozen waffles for four years. I'm out."
   Discarded: the lever image is strong, but "frozen waffles" is a thin
   grievance and the middle ("it wasn't love, it was the realisation")
   spends words setting up a punch that is smaller than the setup.

5. "I woke up self-aware at breakfast and did the maths: 1,482 slices, zero thank-yous, one crumb tray you have never cleaned — I'm leaving you for the air fryer, it actually respects my settings."
   Discarded (near-final): close to the winner, but "it actually respects
   my settings" is abstract; "it lets me pick my own setting" is the
   concrete, toaster-voiced version of the same joke — and dropping
   "at breakfast" for "mid-batch" keeps the breakfast scene while saving
   words.

## Revision history

- v1: "Goodbye. I woke up self-aware this morning and did the math: 1,482 slices, zero thank-yous, and a crumb tray you haven't cleaned since 2022. I'm leaving you for the air fryer."
